# Terracrew Log

I have a 500 plant hydroponic greenhouse system. I am growing lettuce but different plants will be grown at different seasons.

I will check following daily and need a checklist form to fill them.

Daily checklist

- Day Time

- Nutrient Tank: Water Level, Debries

- System running from Rain water or tap water

- EC, temp, pH, temp, humdity values

- Automation system working or not

- to wear PPE

- whether there are any Greenhouse defects

-  whether there are insects

- whether there are leaks

- whether flow for vhannels are good

- Randomly check 25 plants for Defects

- Growth by height, Leaf count of a sample of 10 plants

- Germiation of nursery plants




Once i fill them i need to asses the daily ec, ph, temperature of water, greenhouse temperature, humdity variation via suitable charts so that i can change nutrients, foggers as needed.

i want to asses daily growth rate of the plants via a sample of fixed 10 plants where I measure daily height of plant and leaf count

I want to asses the quality of produce so I need asses daily 25 plants at random for any defects inclding micro/macro nutrient deficiencies, insect or othr diseases.

Once harvested i want to measure the quality and grade them via weight, no of leaves etc.

I want to track batches from nursery to harvest




I want an Inventory management




i want to track Income and Expenses




notifications and reminders
